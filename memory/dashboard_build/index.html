Who can use this dashboard

Choose who can sign in, and what they're allowed to do. For example: giving a colleague access so they can help during an emergency, or removing someone who has left.
Operator — the coordinator at a desk, watching this map and directing help. They can look at the map, look someone up by their rescue code when it comes over the radio, mark someone as found once a team has reached them, ask people "are you still OK?", and download reports for the team. They cannot add or remove anyone, and they cannot set off an alert.
Admin — the person who runs the system. Everything an operator can do, and also: add people to this list or remove them, set off the earthquake alert, and change the logo and authority name that appear on reports.
